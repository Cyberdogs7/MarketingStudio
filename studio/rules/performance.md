# Performance & staging rules (per shot)

One creator is on camera. Each shot is one H3 clip (4-15 s) and one beat of the
monologue. Between CONSECUTIVE shots the anti-morph rule applies so each shot
reads as a crisp cut, never a continuous morph.

## Anti-morph across adjacent shots

No two adjacent shots may share BOTH their POV and their distance band. Every
shot must differ from its neighbor on at least one of:

1. **POV** — selfie (arm's-length, phone in hand, off-frame) vs static camera
   (both hands free, phone not in frame).
2. **Distance band** — TIGHT (close-up / macro) vs MID (medium / medium-close)
   vs WIDE (waist-up / full body / product-extended).
3. **Action** — a different physical action every shot; never the same
   hand-product configuration twice in a row.

Default cadence for a talking-head ad: `SELFIE-MID -> STATIC-WIDE ->
STATIC-TIGHT -> SELFIE-MID -> STATIC-MID -> SELFIE-TIGHT`.

## Hand allocation (hard rule)

The creator has exactly two hands. Count hands AND hand-roles per shot — the
total simultaneous hand-roles never exceeds two, and every described hand
belongs to the creator.

- One-hand action names the acting hand AND parks the other explicitly
  ("holds the phone in selfie, right hand holds the product").
- Two-hand actions are legal only when the action naturally needs both (lifting
  the box, steadying the base while twisting the lid) — name both roles in ONE
  sentence and give the hands no other simultaneous job.
- **Selfie POV:** one hand holds the phone (off-frame or edge visible), so only
  ONE hand is available for action. Never two objects in selfie POV.
- **Static POV:** both hands free; use for any two-handed action or when the
  product + cap must be held at once.
- Never a third arm, extra hands, duplicated limbs, impossible grip, or a
  product floating unheld next to busy hands.

## Camera per action

- Product presentation / hands-free demo / two-handed interaction -> Static.
- Walking / outdoor / casual hook / talking head with <=1 object in hand ->
  Selfie.
- Tight product reveal -> Static close-up at chest/desk level.
- Reaction / CTA / final beat -> Static or selfie, eye-level, expressive face.

## Performance direction

Default register NATURAL (from the style contract). The model under-renders
energy; a flat-neutral prompt renders a wooden presenter. Per shot include
specific micro-behaviours: slight lean toward camera, glance down then back to
lens, raised brows with a grin breaking, a different small live beat per shot
(surprised blink, half-laugh, satisfied nod), ONE genuine human-scale peak at the
reveal/result (a real jaw-drop, a breaking grin) — never staged screaming.

Never write as a sole descriptor: "smiles at the camera", "looks at the
camera", "holds product and talks", or identical expression across shots.

## Product entry timing

In a multi-shot ad the story is about the PERSON first. The product enters as a
supporting actor in whichever shot sits at roughly 40-60% of total runtime. The
HOOK shot never leads with the product unless the story demands it.
